# Code of Conduct

Be respectful. Harassment, personal attacks, and bad-faith disruption are not welcome.

This project follows a short, practical standard:

- Assume good intent, then be direct.
- Critique ideas, not people.
- Do not publish others' private information.
- Do not use the project spaces to spam or advertise unrelated products.

Maintainers may warn, restrict, or remove people who ignore this standard.

Report conduct issues privately to the maintainers.
